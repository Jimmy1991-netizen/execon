Short Background

The trip from Sikatuna Municipality as entry point to the Loboc Heritage Building, where the executive conference will be held, offers a brief yet scenic journey through the heart of Loboc. Travelers will pass by Tambis Elementary School, a landmark before the route leads directly to the Loboc Heritage Building.
